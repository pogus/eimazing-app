Eimazing

Personal content-automation tools that help the owner create and publish their own videos and print-on-demand designs.

Eimazing is a set of private, single-user tools operated by the owner of the Eimazings Etsy shop. The tools are not offered to the public and never act on behalf of other users.

Pin scheduler (Pinterest)

Schedules and publishes product pins to the owner's own Pinterest business account using the official Pinterest API. Each pin is an image of one of the owner's own shop products, posted to the owner's own boards with a link back to the product listing. Planned endpoints: pins:write, boards:read and boards:write on the owner's account only; no other Pinterest users ever authenticate with or use the tool.

Pi Uploader (YouTube)

Uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API.

Pi Uploader (TikTok)

Publishes the owner's own short vertical videos — the same videos the owner produces for YouTube, plus short promotional clips for the owner's own shop products — to the owner's own TikTok account using the official TikTok Content Posting API. Each video is created by the owner, given a title, caption and hashtags, and uploaded to the owner's own account only. Planned scopes: video.upload (and video.publish for direct posting) with user.info.basic, on the owner's account only; no other TikTok users ever authenticate with or use the tool, and the tool never reads other users' content.
