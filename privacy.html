Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

Eimazing ("the App") is a set of personal automation tools operated by their owner to manage the owner's own content: videos on the owner's YouTube channel and product pins for the owner's own Etsy shop (Eimazings). This policy explains what data the App handles. The video component is also known as "Pi Uploader".

Who this applies to

The App is used solely by its owner to access the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, does not create accounts for other users, and never acts on behalf of anyone but the owner. No visitor or customer data is collected — this website is a static page with no cookies, analytics, or tracking.

What the App accesses — Pinterest

With the owner's explicit consent through Pinterest OAuth, the App uses the official Pinterest API to:

  • create and schedule pins of the owner's own shop products on the owner's own boards (pins:write);
  • read and manage the owner's own boards (boards:read, boards:write); and
  • read basic account information needed to perform those actions (user_accounts:read).

The App never reads, stores, or interacts with content or data belonging to any other Pinterest user.

What the App accesses — Google/YouTube

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the App uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel; and
  • read basic channel information needed to perform those uploads (youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes).

How data is stored and used

OAuth tokens granted by Pinterest and Google are stored locally on the owner's own devices and are used only to authenticate actions on the owner's own accounts. The App does not sell, share, or transfer this data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are retained only while the App is in use and can be revoked at any time by the owner: Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and Pinterest access in the owner's Pinterest security settings (Connected apps). Revoking immediately ends the App's access.

Platform policies

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of the Pinterest API adheres to the Pinterest Developer Guidelines.
