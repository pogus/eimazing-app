User Data Deletion

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Eimazing ("the App", including the Meta app "Eimazings Publisher") is a set of personal automation tools that only acts on its owner's own accounts. It stores no accounts, profiles or personal data of other people. The only data it keeps is the owner's own access tokens and the IDs of posts and comments it has handled on the owner's own accounts.

Remove the App's access (Facebook / Instagram)

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Find Eimazings Publisher and choose Remove.

This immediately ends the App's access.

Request deletion of any data

If you have interacted with our Page or account (for example by commenting) and want any data related to you deleted, email pontusgustavsson99@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete any stored record related to you, such as the ID of a comment we replied to, within 30 days and confirm by email.
